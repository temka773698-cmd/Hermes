## Stage 2 — after deployment to the production domain
1. Upload the contents of `release-reg-ru/` to the production host.
2. Open these URLs in a browser and confirm HTTP 200:
   - `/robots.txt`
   - `/sitemap.xml`
   - `/contacts.html`
   - `/privacy-policy.html`
3. In Yandex Webmaster:
   - add the site;
   - verify site ownership;
   - submit sitemap.xml;
   - set regional relevance to Kazan if prompted;
   - monitor index coverage and title/snippet issues.

## Stage 3 — Yandex Business / local presence
1. Create or update the business card in Yandex Business.
2. Use the same phone number and site URL as on the website.
3. Add categories relevant to wells / water systems / heating.
4. Fill service area: Kazan and nearby settlements.
5. Add real photos of objects, works, equipment, before/after if available.
6. Collect and respond to reviews.
